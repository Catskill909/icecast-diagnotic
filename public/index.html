info KPFT Stream Monitor — User Guide
cell_tower

Real-Time Stream Monitoring

Monitors KPFT Main (128k), HD2, and HD3 streams every 60 seconds. Checks HTTP status, latency (ms), and content-type headers.

volume_off

Smart Silence & Dead Air Engine

Adaptive multi-stage engine prevents false alarms during talk show speech pauses. Silence triggers rapid 5s re-verification probes (~32KB sample); any detected voice or audio energy instantly resets the streak.

group

Icecast Server Statistics

Connects directly to Icecast /status-json.xsl to track active listener counts, peak listeners, and broadcast bitrates in real time.

troubleshoot

Root Cause Diagnosis

Every failure is diagnosed, not just reported. Correlates the DNS/TCP/TLS timing breakdown against Icecast's live mount inventory to tell a source encoder dropout (mount 404s but the server is healthy — a studio problem) apart from a genuine server outage. Also checks whether other Pacifica stations are still streaming to confirm whether the fault is ours.

mail

Smart Email Alerts

Alerts staff when a stream fails 2 consecutive checks, or immediately if a single failure hits every stream at once (server-level). Emails lead with the root cause and include specific remediation steps. Every event records whether its alert actually sent — a failed SMTP delivery is now visible rather than silent.

history

Permanent Incident History

Every failed check is recorded forever — including brief blips that self-clear before triggering an alert, which previously left a red mark on the uptime bar with no record anywhere. Open History for the full filterable timeline, outage heatmap, and per-event drill-down.

play_circle

In-Dashboard Preview Player

Click the play button on any stream card to listen to live audio directly in your browser. Features a buffering indicator and animated equalizer waves.
