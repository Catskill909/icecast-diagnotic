troubleshoot

It tells you which end broke — the studio or the server

This is the point of the tool. A dropout is not just reported, it is diagnosed. Every check compares the stream against Icecast's live mount inventory and against the other streams, so an alert names the cause instead of leaving you to guess who to call.

🎙️ Studio side — the Barix encoder The mount returns 404 and has vanished from Icecast's inventory, while the server keeps serving every other station normally. The encoder has dropped its connection. Everyone listening is cut off and the audience takes a long time to rebuild.
🖥️ Server side — Icecast / Pacifica The connection is reset or refused, often on several streams at the same second. People already listening are usually unaffected, but new listeners cannot start playback during the window.
cell_tower

Real-Time Monitoring

Checks KPFT Main, HD2 and HD3 every 60 seconds — HTTP status, content type, and a full DNS → TCP → TLS → first byte timing breakdown, which reveals where in the chain a failure happened. Press play on any stream card to confirm by ear what the monitor is reporting.

bolt

Outages vs. Blips

A single failed check is a blip; two in a row is a confirmed outage. Both are recorded permanently, but only confirmed outages email — blips are usually over before anyone could act. Blips used to leave a red mark on the uptime bar with no record at all.

volume_off

Silence & Dead Air Detection

Catches the case where the stream is connected but silent. To avoid false alarms during speech pauses, suspected silence triggers rapid 5-second re-probes; any detected audio instantly resets the count. Dead air is only declared after sustained silence.

mail

Actionable Email Alerts

Alerts lead with the root cause and list specific things to check. A failure hitting every stream at once is server-level by definition and alerts immediately. Each event also records whether its alert actually sent — a failed delivery is visible rather than silent.

history

Permanent History

Incidents are kept forever, not wiped after 24 hours. Open History for the full timeline with filters, an outage heatmap, a root-cause breakdown, and a drill-down on every event showing its evidence and timings.

group

Listener Impact

Live listener counts come straight from Icecast. When a mount disappears the count correctly drops to zero rather than holding its last value — so the real audience loss during an outage is visible instead of hidden.
