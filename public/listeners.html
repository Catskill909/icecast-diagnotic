groups Listener Numbers
info
Total listeners counts how many times someone tuned in — every rise in the listener count is somebody starting to listen. It is the reach figure: on this station it runs six to nine times the number listening at once. It is a floor, because within one check someone leaving as someone else arrives cancels out and is invisible; the real number is higher, never lower. One person who tunes in ten times counts ten times — how many different people that was is the separate figure beside it.

At once is the most connected at a single moment, every channel summed — a fact about server load rather than reach. Typically is the average.

Each period is compared against the same elapsed span of the one before it, so nine days of this month are measured against the first nine days of last month rather than against all thirty-one.

alt_route By Mount
info
A channel is published at several bitrates, each its own Icecast mount, and the audience splits across them. This is the only place that split is visible — every other figure sums them. It reaches back as far as raw samples are kept; older buckets compact the breakdown away.
schedule By Hour of Day
info
Average listeners at each hour across the whole retained record, not just the selected range — the point of the shape is that it holds across many days. Shown in your browser's local time.
hourglass_top Listening Hours (ATH)
info
One ATH is one person listening for one hour. For a US noncommercial webcaster it is the figure the SoundExchange royalty rate is computed from — the annual fee covers each channel's first 159,140 ATH per month, and more is owed above that. These are estimates, derived from counting listeners once a minute rather than from a log of every connection, so treat them as an early warning and get the real figure before filing anything.
devices Who Is Listening
help
Measured from Icecast's own per-connection record, not inferred from polling. Session length is real — Icecast reports how long each connection has lasted — so this is the only place the app can state time spent listening rather than estimate it.

Machines are separated from people. A connection open for longer than 12 hours is a relay or a scraper, not a listener, and is excluded from the audience figure while still being counted.

Aggregators may hide the majority. Where TuneIn and similar proxy their listeners, one connection stands for many and carries no device detail — so the breakdown describes the direct-connection audience.

Requires sign-in. No listener address or raw device string is ever sent to this page; everything here is counted on the server.
